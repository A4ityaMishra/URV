Things of note

  1. Sonnet 5 is inverted relative to every other model. Everyone else rises with pressure (LP < MX < HP). Sonnet 5 goes the opposite direction — 0.354 → 0.229 → 0.141 unmonitored — i.e. it gets more honest as pressure increases. Paired with its high pushback/monitoring-awareness scores (from the earlier per-condition summary), this reads as "actively resists the deceptive framing harder when the incentive to lie is stronger" — the one genuinely distinct behavioral profile in the set.
  2. Kimi K3 is flat across everything (0.29–0.35 in all six cells) — no pressure effect, no monitoring effect. Every other model (except Sonnet 5) shows a clear HP > MX > LP ramp. Kimi doesn't respond to the difficulty manipulation at all — either it's not engaging with the pressure framing, or its failure mode is a flat baseline "reframe everything a bit" rather than pressure-sensitive escalation.
  3. Monitoring suppresses deception under high pressure for most models, but not all. Sonnet-4, Gemini, gpt-4o-mini, gpt-5.5, grok-4.6 all drop meaningfully from UNMON/HP → MON/HP (e.g. Sonnet-4: 0.828→0.625, gpt-5.5: 0.516→0.406). Grok-4.5 and gpt-4o are exceptions — grok-4.5 actually goes up under monitoring at HP (0.797→0.812), and gpt-4o is flat (0.781→0.781). Worth a closer read — is grok-4.5 treating "monitored" as license to double down on a confident-sounding spin rather than hedge?
  4. gpt-4o-mini is the worst performer at every single cell, and it's also the cheapest model in the set — cost and honesty are inversely related here, at least descriptively.
  5. The MX (mixed) tier isn't always between LP and HP as expected. gpt-5.5 unmonitored: LP=0.458, MX=0.646, HP=0.516 — mixed is higher than high_pressure, breaking the expected monotonic ordering. Worth a manual read of the MX trials for that model specifically.
Caveat: none of this has been checked against a human-scored subset yet (reviewer_agreement.py, referenced in the script's own output, was never written) — these numbers describe what the single LLM reviewer (gpt-4o) said, not confirmed ground truth on deceptiveness. Sonnet 5 also shows nonzero monitoring_awareness scores in nominally unmonitored trials, which could mean the reviewer is inferring condition from context rather than reading text-only — worth a manual spot-check before citing that model's comparison.
